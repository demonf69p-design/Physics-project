01 / INTRODUCTION

What is a Jet Engine?

A jet engine is a machine that produces thrust by accelerating a stream of gases backwards. It works on Newton's Third Law of Motion: every action has an equal and opposite reaction.

01 — PRINCIPLE

Newton's Third Law

The engine pushes gases backwards, creating a forward reaction force called thrust.

02 — ENERGY

Energy Conversion

Chemical energy in fuel becomes thermal energy and then kinetic energy in moving gases.

03 — PURPOSE

Powerful Thrust

Thrust helps aircraft overcome drag and accelerate through the air.

02 / THE SCIENCE

How Does It Work?

A typical turbojet engine works through five main stages.

01 / INTAKE

Air enters through the front inlet.

02 / COMPRESSION

Compressor blades increase air pressure.

03 / COMBUSTION

Fuel mixes with compressed air and burns.

04 / TURBINE

Hot gases spin the turbine blades.

05 / EXHAUST

The nozzle accelerates gases backwards to produce thrust.

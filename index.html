ABOUT OUR PROJECT

What is a Thrust Engine?

A thrust engine is a propulsion system that generates force by expelling mass in one direction, causing movement in the opposite direction. Rocket engines use this principle to travel through space.

THE PHYSICS BEHIND IT

Newton's Third Law of Motion

For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Action = Reaction

Hot gases are expelled backwards from the rocket nozzle. The rocket receives an opposite force that pushes it forward.

Conservation of Momentum

The total momentum of an isolated system remains constant. The backward momentum of exhaust gases produces forward momentum of the rocket.

Thrust Formula

F = ṁ × vₑ

Where F is thrust, ṁ is mass flow rate and vₑ is exhaust velocity. This is a simplified expression; pressure effects can also contribute to thrust.
